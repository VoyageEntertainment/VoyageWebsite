Work with us

Retention before scale

We read day one and day seven retention before spending anything on traffic. Games that do not hold players do not get promoted.

Weekly content cadence

New units, events and limited drops on a fixed schedule. Players learn when to come back, and the update itself becomes the marketing.

Monetization that fits the loop

Passes and products built around what players already want to do faster, rather than paywalls dropped in front of core progression.

Community and moderation

Acquisitions

Selling your Roblox game.

If you have built something that works but you do not want to keep running it, we will take it over. Here is the sequence from first message to handover.

  1. 01

    You send the details

    Share the game link plus whatever analytics you have: visits, concurrent players, retention and revenue history.

  2. 02

    We review and make an offer

    We look at the numbers, play the game and check how much of the codebase is yours, then come back with a written offer.

  3. 03

    Upfront, or revenue share

    Take the full amount at closing and walk away, or take less upfront and keep a share of what the game earns afterwards.

  4. 04

    Handover and first update

    We move the experience, source and assets across, then ship the first update under our team. You are done.

What we look for

  • Live and playable

    Published on Roblox with real players in it, not a prototype or an unreleased build.

  • Yours to sell

    You own the place and the code, or you can name every contributor and the terms they worked under.

  • Any genre, any size

    Simulators, tycoons, obbies, survival. Small games with a strong loop are worth a conversation.

Questions

Before you get in touch.

What creators ask us most. For anything else, write to voyagenoirtype@gmail.com.

What do you need from me to make an offer?

The game link and access to your analytics. Visit history, concurrent player graphs, retention and revenue over the last few months are what actually drive the number. If you do not have all of it, send what you have and we will tell you what is missing.

How is the offer calculated?

Mainly from recent revenue and how stable it is, adjusted for how much work the game needs before it can scale. A title with flat revenue and clean code is worth more to us than one with a spike from a single viral video. We explain the reasoning with every offer.

Can I keep a share instead of selling outright?

Yes. You can take a lower payment at closing and keep an agreed percentage of net revenue afterwards. It suits creators who believe the game has room to grow and want exposure to that without doing the work.

What happens to the game after the handover?

It goes on our update schedule. We keep the core loop that made it work and build on it rather than rewriting the game into something players do not recognise. Existing purchases and player progress are preserved.
