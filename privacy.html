Privacy

What this site collects.

A plain description of every piece of data this website handles and every third party it contacts. Last reviewed November 2026.

  • No cookies, no tracking

    No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts run on this site.

  • The contact form

    Delivered to our inbox through Formspree and used only to reply to you.

  • Kept in your browser

    A short-lived copy of the public game statistics, and nothing else.

  • Services contacted

    Google Fonts, Roblox by way of four proxies, and Formspree.

Analytics and cookies

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. We do not build a profile of you and we have no visitor dashboard.

The contact form

When you submit the form on the contact section, the name, email address, game link and message you typed are sent to Formspree, which delivers them to our inbox. Formspree processes the submission on our behalf and its own privacy policy applies to that step.

We use what you send only to reply to you and to evaluate a game if you are offering one. We do not sell it, and we do not add you to a mailing list. If you want your message and our copy of it deleted, email us and we will remove it.

The form asks you to confirm that you are 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Data stored in your browser

To avoid re-fetching the same numbers on every page view, the site keeps a copy of the public Roblox statistics in your browser under the local storage key voyage_live_stats. It expires after 30 minutes, contains no personal information, and never leaves your device. Clearing your site data removes it.

Third parties your browser contacts

  • Google Fonts serves the typeface from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Your IP address is visible to Google as part of that request.
  • Roblox provides the visit, favorite and concurrent player counts through its public games API.
  • Proxies relay that Roblox request, because the API does not allow direct browser calls. The site tries games.roproxy.com, a mirror of the Roblox API, along with corsproxy.io, api.allorigins.win and api.codetabs.com, and uses whichever answers first. These requests carry no personal data beyond what any HTTP request exposes.
  • Formspree receives contact form submissions, as described above.

Nothing on this page is under our control once it reaches those services, so their own policies govern what they do with it.

Links to Roblox

Game cards link out to roblox.com. Once you follow one of those links you are on Roblox and its terms and privacy policy apply. Voyage Interactive is not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
